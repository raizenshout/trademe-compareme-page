Built for Properties

Scrapes rent, property type, available date, maximum tenants, description, address, and embeds direct Google Maps routing to help you find your next home.

🚗

Tailored for Motors

Extracts odometer, body style, transmission, seats, fuel type, plate, year, owners, import history, and displays WOF and Registration status instantly.

🔒

100% Local & Private

All listings and comparison data are stored on your device in local storage. No trackers, no remote databases, no registration. Completely private.

Compact List View

Keep track of your active candidates in a tidy list, automatically sorted by category (Properties, Motors, Other).

  • Access your saved lists directly from the toolbar
  • Live updates as you save listings on active tabs
  • Quick removal of outdated options

Side-by-Side Comparison Matrix

Open the full comparison view in a new tab. All specs are aligned side-by-side, so differences in mileage, price, or location stand out immediately.

  • Auto-highlighted discrepancies
  • Full spec grids (WOF, plate, transmission, rent date)
  • Expandable descriptions for quick reading
